## How We Use AI Responsibly

Everyone is moving to adopt AI, and 95% of businesses get nothing out of it (MIT NANDA). Doing it right starts with clear boundaries.

- **Our data never trains someone else's model.** Every model we use runs with training on our data turned off.
- **Sensitive records are walled off from AI entirely.** Protected folders the AI cannot read, search, or reason about, enforced by the system itself, not by an instruction.
- **A person approves what matters.** Anything that reaches a client, changes configuration, or adds a connection waits for a clear yes.
- **Every action leaves a record.** Each AI action is written to an audit log as it happens, and sealed at the end of the session.
- **Risk is tracked and corrected.** A register of 41 known AI risks, each with an owner and a mitigation plan. When a safeguard blocks an action, a separate safeguard logs the incident automatically, and every incident gets a corrective action report. Average close time is under a day.

Full detail in our [Responsible AI report](https://maidenops.com/blog/2026-10-19-responsible-ai).
